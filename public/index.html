Boba Rush

Brew it. Shake it. Serve it. Together.

or play with friends

Multiplayer needs the Boba Rush server. Run npm start and open http://localhost:3000.

How to play
  1. Counter: take a customer's order to print a ticket.
  2. Brew: pick the tea, then hold Pour and release at the fill line.
  3. Mix: pump the sweetness and scoop the ice they asked for.
  4. Toppings: add exactly what is on the ticket.
  5. Shake & Serve: stop the needle in the green zone, then serve.

Faster and more accurate drinks earn bigger tips. In co-op, everyone shares the shop: claim tickets so you don't double up. Keys: 1-5 switch stations, Space pours and stops the shaker.
